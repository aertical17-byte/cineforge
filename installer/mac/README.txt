CineForge AI — first launch
===========================

1) Drag "CineForge AI" into the Applications folder (arrow on the right).
2) Open the app and click "Set up & start CineForge on my Mac".
   You need Docker Desktop (free): if it's missing, the app takes you to the
   download page. The first launch downloads and builds: 5-15 minutes.
   Watch the progress in the window.
3) When it's ready http://localhost:3000 opens — you're in right away:
   no signup (automatic guest session), unlimited credits
   (1,000,000 welcome ones, never expiring).
4) In any studio pick the engine "AI Horde · community (free, no key)":
   it generates AI images at zero cost (no card, no signup).

If macOS says the app "cannot be opened" (normal: no paid Apple signature):
- System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway" → Open, or
- in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/CineForge AI.app"

To stop everything: open the app → "Stop services".
Full guide and troubleshooting: guida.html inside the app ("Guide" button).
