🎬 CineForge AI — step by step

This guide works even without internet (except the links). The same guide, always up to date, lives in the GitHub project.

1 · Requirements

✅ macOS 13 or later

✅ Docker Desktop (free) — the only thing to install: it runs CineForge on your Mac. The app opens it for you if it finds it, and takes you to the download if it's missing.

2 · First launch (only once)

  1. Open CineForge AI (from Applications).
  2. Click “Set up & start CineForge on my Mac” and choose the folder to keep the project in (e.g. Documents).
  3. The app downloads the code and builds the services. The first launch takes 5–15 minutes (it downloads a lot: that's normal). Watch the details in the window.
  4. When it's ready http://localhost:3000 opens in your browser.

3 · If macOS says the app “cannot be opened”

Normal: the app doesn't carry Apple's paid signature (€99/year). You can open it safely — it's open source:

  • System Settings → Privacy & Security → at the bottom “Open Anyway” → Open, or
  • Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/CineForge AI.app"
  • On macOS 14 or older: right-click the icon → Open → Open also works (once is enough)

4 · Create your first image (free)

  1. Open localhost:3000 — you're already in: no signup (an automatic guest session) and unlimited credits (1,000,000 welcome ones, never expiring).
  2. Create a project → open an Image studio.
  3. Pick the engine “AI Horde · community (free, no key)”.
  4. Write the prompt (e.g. “a rain-wet street, neon reflections”) and press Generate.
💸 Cost: zero. The community engine uses volunteers' GPUs (open project AI Horde): sometimes you wait 30–120 seconds in a queue, and that's the price of free. It makes ~512 px images, perfect for storyboards and drafts. Video requires an optional paid engine (your own key).

5 · Stop and restart

  • Stop everything: open the app → “Stop services”.
  • Restart: “Set up & start” (fast after the first time).
  • Update to the latest version: in Terminal: cd ~/Documents/cineforge && git pull then “Set up & start”.

6 · Common problems

❌ “Docker didn't start” → open Docker Desktop from the menu bar (the 🐳 whale), wait until it's green, then try again.

❌ “Startup failed” → reopen Docker, check you have ~6 GB free on disk, and try again. Technical details are in the app window.

❌ “localhost:3000 doesn't respond after 20 minutes” → the build may continue in the background: after a few minutes click “Open the app in your browser”.

❌ Port already in use → another app is using 3000/8000: close it, or in Terminal docker compose down and try again.
